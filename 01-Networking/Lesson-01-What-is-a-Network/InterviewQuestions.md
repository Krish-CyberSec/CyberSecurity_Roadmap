# Lesson 01 Interview Questions

## Beginner

### Q1. What is a computer network?

Answer:

...
computer network is a collection of interconnected devices that communicate and exchange data over wired or wireless media using standardised communication protocols. Its primary purpose is to enable communication, resource sharing, and access to services.
---

### Q2. Why do computers need protocols?

Answer:

...
Computers need protocols because they define the rules for communication. They specify how data is formatted, transmitted, received, and interpreted so that different devices can understand each other, even if they are made by different manufacturers.
---

## Intermediate

### Q3. Why is networking important for cybersecurity?

Answer:

...
  Almost every cyberattack involves communication between systems. Understanding networking enables security professionals to identify how attackers gain access, move laterally within a network, communicate with malicious servers, and exfiltrate sensitive data. Without networking knowledge, investigating and defending against attacks becomes significantly more difficult. 
---

## Advanced

### Q4. Explain networking to a CEO.

Answer:

...
Imagine your company has offices across India. Employees need roads, courier services, and agreed delivery rules to send documents between offices. A computer network works the same way. It connects devices and ensures information reaches the correct destination securely and efficiently. Without a network, employees cannot access applications, send emails, or communicate with company systems.
